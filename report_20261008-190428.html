1. What changed since the previous TSM underwriting?

Resolved the key open demand checkpoint. Prior research had flagged a September sales threshold of roughly NT$445B–NT$483B to place Q3 within the dollar guidance range at management's NT$32/USD assumption. Actual September revenue of NT$511.86B surpassed even the high-end threshold. The operating backdrop has therefore improved versus the pre-release uncertainty. [1][3]

4. Thesis assessment

What is working

  • Strength with acceleration: reported sales growth improved from +44.7% in July to +53.3% in August and +54.6% in September.
  • Structural positioning: TSMC's advanced-node, scale, yield and packaging ecosystem remains difficult to replicate; leading-edge nodes represented 77% of wafer revenue in Q2.
  • Capacity optionality: a separate GlobalFoundries–TSMC five-year silicon-interposer agreement supports prospective U.S. advanced-packaging supply, with volume ramp planned in 1H28, not a near-term Q3 revenue driver. [6]

What remains exposed

  • Margins: earlier 3Q gross-margin guidance was 65%–67%, down from 67.7% in Q2; N2 and overseas expansions may create transitional dilution.
  • Cash conversion: an elevated capital program makes free cash flow more demanding than headline earnings.
  • Customer concentration and cyclicality: AI infrastructure demand may slow or pull forward shipments; monthlies cannot identify end-customer inventory.
  • Geopolitical and macro risk: Taiwan operations, trade restrictions and high discount rates remain material.

5. Valuation and portfolio-manager interpretation

At a US$455.62 intraday reference price as of approximately 2:44 p.m. EDT on October 8, public market data showed about 29.7x trailing earnings, 20.3x vendor-defined forward earnings, and 57.8x trailing free cash flow—materially different valuations because capex depresses near-term free cash flow. The vendor does not publish a usable PEG number in this snapshot; no PEG-based buy conclusion is warranted. These metrics and the quote are time-stamped and will change. [5]

Investment implicationThe release supports retaining exposure to TSMC's leading-edge manufacturing franchise. Following the intraday pullback into the prior research's provisional US$450–460 staged-add band, an investor could re-evaluate a small incremental purchase conditional on the October 15 margin/guide confirmation and total semiconductor exposure. This is not a new independently recalculated price target, buy order, or Portfolio CIO rating.

Scenario discipline: In a bull case, Q4 guidance and 2027 commitments validate accelerating AI/HPC revenue with better-than-feared margins; in the base case, elevated growth persists but new-node and overseas dilution temper near-term margins; in a bear case, AI orders normalize against expanded capex while lower FCF and higher yields compress the multiple. Today’s revenue evidence modestly increases confidence in the demand side of the bull/base cases, not in the profitability or valuation assumptions.

6. Next checkpoints and thesis-break conditions

Immediate proof points

  • October 15, 2026 Q3 results: GAAP and operating margins versus 65%–67% and 56%–58% guidance; N2 yield and mix; Q4 forecast and capex.
  • November 10 monthly report: October momentum versus an elevated September base.
  • 2027 capacity visibility: advanced packaging, contracted leading-edge demand and return on the investment program.

Escalate for CIO review if

  • Multiple quarters of demand-driven leading-edge slowdown and reduced customer production plans.
  • Gross margins remain below ~56%–58% through cycle, without a credible temporary explanation.
  • N2 qualification or yield delays cause lasting margin/volume pressure; overseas ramp dilutes more than planned.
  • Peak capex persists while utilization and FCF deteriorate, implying potential overbuild.
Important disclosureThis is a monthly-sales event report, not a Q3 earnings release. Gross profit, EPS, reported operating cash flow, segment contribution and management's updated guidance were not released with these September figures. Consequently, it is premature to issue a fully underwritten new fair value or a formal upgrade based on the release alone.
