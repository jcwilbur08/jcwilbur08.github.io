Highest-priority opportunities

1. UBER - best tactical asymmetry

The Sep. 19 re-underwrite estimates an illustrative probability-weighted value around $90 versus a $70.50 close, with a $55 bear / $93 base / $120 bull framework. The core business continues to show strong bookings growth, operating leverage and FCF conversion. The position is only 0.063%, leaving room for staged construction. The reason not to be aggressive is AV: more than $10B of expected commitments raises capital-return and disintermediation risk.

2. GEHC - undervaluation with execution gates

At $64.13, GEHC still offers a meaningful valuation gap to prior scenario work while adding healthcare diversification. The position remains small enough that portfolio fit is favorable. The required proof points are PCS repair, stronger cash conversion, backlog conversion and disciplined M&A.

3. TSM - preferred direct semiconductor

TSM retains the strongest blend of process leadership, AI/HPC demand, scale economics and valuation among the semiconductor choices in the researched universe. The stock closed at $433.52, above the prior intraday snapshot, so staging is preferable. Taiwan geopolitical risk remains the non-diversifiable thesis risk.

4-6. VXUS / AVUV / AVDV - strategic diversifiers

These are less about a near-term catalyst and more about improving the portfolio's expected-return mix. VXUS broadens geographic exposure, AVUV adds U.S. small-value/profitability, and AVDV adds international small-value/profitability. Their ranking stays high because the portfolio remains concentrated in U.S. large-cap financial, payment and technology exposures.

7. ADBE - quality at a reset valuation

Adobe offers a high-margin, cash-generative software franchise at a materially reset valuation and is not currently held. The opportunity hinges on generative AI proving additive to Creative Cloud / Acrobat monetization rather than structurally eroding seat value or competitive differentiation.

8. VRTX - diversification improves relative rank

VRTX moves up because it supplies high-quality healthcare growth without adding to the portfolio's dominant factor exposures. The stock's modest Friday decline improves relative entry value. The principal risks are pipeline concentration and the valuation assigned to assets beyond the cystic-fibrosis franchise.

Why the AI / power names are not higher

NVDA

Outstanding operating thesis, but Friday closed at $222.04 and the direct position increased to 29 shares. Prior underwriting still shows a better margin of safety below roughly $205. Additions should be smaller than they would be at lower prices.

AVGO / ASML

Both remain high-quality strategic assets. The issue is marginal utility: AVGO is already 2.49% of the portfolio and ASML closed at $1,679.25 after a strong Friday. Their franchises are more attractive than their next-dollar urgency.

GEV / GRID

The power and grid thesis remains structurally strong. GEV is near its latest base/scenario values, while GRID is above the preferred add zone. Better entry prices would move both materially higher without requiring a change in the underlying thesis.

Implementation framework

Priority capital
UBER, GEHC, TSM, VXUS, AVUV, AVDV and ADBE. These have the best combination of expected return, strategic fit and current-price support. Use staged entries rather than treating the ranking as a one-day trade list.
Selective capital
VRTX, NVDA, LOW, ASML, CRM, UNH and PANW. All are investable under the current research view, but either valuation, existing exposure or a specific execution checkpoint reduces urgency.
Attractive but gated
AVGO, AXP, QCOM, VIS and LMT. The main constraint is portfolio concentration or a less compelling current-price expected return, not necessarily weak company quality.
Wait / monitor
GEV and GRID. Both are structurally attractive themes, but current prices do not compensate enough for valuation and rate risk relative to higher-ranked alternatives.

What could change the ranking quickly?

Move higher

  • NVDA below roughly $205 without a thesis break.
  • GEV into roughly the $850-$875 zone with backlog and margin evidence intact.
  • GRID returning to the preferred accumulation zone.
  • ASML / AVGO pullbacks without estimate deterioration.
  • Further evidence that UBER's AV capital commitments earn attractive returns through third-party financing and platform economics.

Move lower

  • 10-year yields sustaining materially above 5% with no offsetting earnings revisions.
  • UBER AV commitments accelerating while platform economics deteriorate.
  • GEHC PCS / FCF execution missing the current checkpoints.
  • Further semiconductor additions elsewhere in the portfolio, raising concentration even if company-level theses remain intact.
  • A significant oil / inflation shock that pushes the Fed toward a more restrictive path.
