§1 · The verdict in one page

Everything else is discipline. Alphabet's post-print washout earns a starter zone only, with the deeper $240–250 level named. Adobe adds happen at the ≤$235 gate or not at all. Qualcomm's −11.1% Friday fall was Intel contagion, not earnings — but its own print is November 4, so it waits. And a full third of the universe gets no new money at current prices, each for a stated reason: above target (LLY, V, XOM), entry level far away (HD at ≤$260, LOW at ≤$165, COST at ≤$850), or a rating under review (BMY).

§2 · How this ranking is built

Conviction-weighted upside, tempered by entry discipline and the weight still to build.

Method note

The desk ranks on conviction-weighted upside — rating conviction × upside to target — tempered by entry discipline (no adds above a standing entry level, and no adds into an imminent print) and by current weight versus target weight. Cam's October 15 ranking weighted position-building priority instead, which put UBER and TSM first. The two methods converge at the top this week because Friday's selloff put AVGO, UBER, and NVDA all inside entry at once. Where a name's price is above its entry level or its print is imminent, it is demoted to Tier 2 or Tier 3 regardless of its raw score — GEHC, with +20.1% upside and 7/10 conviction, ranks 21st for exactly that reason.
