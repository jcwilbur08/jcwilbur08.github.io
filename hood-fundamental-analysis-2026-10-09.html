Investment call

Buy the operating trajectory. Respect the entry price.

Robinhood has graduated from a single-product trading app into a fast-scaling financial platform. The evidence is visible in record deposits, rising assets and a broader earnings base—but a 47.5× trailing P/E and roughly 20–22× sales leave little room for a merely good quarter.

Why the business earns a Buy

The platform reached 28.4 million funded customers, $369 billion of platform assets and a record $21.7 billion of quarterly net deposits in Q2. Those deposits equate to roughly 28% annualized organic growth. Thirteen business lines now exceed $100 million in annualized revenue, and Q2 revenue rose 32% even as crypto transaction revenue fell 38%.

Best-in-class reported economics strengthen the case: a 42.0% net margin, 22.4% return on equity and a 57% adjusted EBITDA margin in Q2 show that operating leverage is already present rather than merely promised.

Desk verdict

Buy, 6/10 conviction. The $130 twelve-month target implies 19.2% upside and sits modestly below the roughly $133–134 sell-side average.

Recommended—not installed: add at or below $98 toward an approximately 1% portfolio weight. That level is about 10% below the reference price and improves the margin of safety ahead of earnings.

Why conviction stops at 6

Morningstar initiated coverage with a narrow moat, Very High uncertainty and a $50 fair value estimate, calling the shares very expensive. The stock also carries a 2.47 beta, the richest sales multiple in its peer set, continued crypto sensitivity and an active multi-state challenge to prediction markets. With Q3 earnings only 18 days away—and a 13–14% drop after the Q1 double miss—this is not a name to chase into the print.

Business engine

Diversification is becoming visible in the income statement.

The platform still monetizes trading intensity, but options, equities, net interest, subscriptions, event contracts and newer financial products now share the load. Q2 was the cleanest proof yet that growth can continue while crypto contracts.

Trading

Q2 transaction revenue reached $776 million: options $342 million, equities $129 million, crypto $100 million and event contracts $156 million.

Net interest income contributed $389 million in Q2. Other revenue reached $143 million, supported by Gold and Trump Account service revenue.

Gold Card, Legend, Banking, prediction markets, retirement, tokenization, AI agents and international crypto broaden the customer relationship.

FY2025 revenue rose 52% to $4.5 billion. Adjusted EBITDA increased 76% to $2.522 billion at a 56% margin; net income reached $1.883 billion and diluted EPS was $2.05. All were company records.

Outlook & roadmap

Management guides expenses; product velocity carries the rest.

What would move conviction higher

A clean Q3 beat driven by customer assets, deposits and recurring revenue—not only trading intensity—combined with disciplined expenses and a lower-risk regulatory path for event contracts. The valuation requires evidence that platform breadth can produce durable growth through multiple market regimes.

Risk register

Fast growth is colliding with fast regulation.

High · immediate

Prediction-market enforcement

Michigan required Robinhood Derivatives to stop new sports contracts and close positions by October 9. Ohio issued cease-and-desist letters on October 2, with responses due October 16, and Connecticut was pushing platforms to exit. Conflicting federal rulings keep the preemption question unsettled.

High · cyclical

Crypto sensitivity

Crypto revenue fell 38–47% year over year for three consecutive quarters. Diversification softened the Q2 impact, but crypto remains the dominant swing factor in sentiment and transaction economics.

High · valuation

Multiple compression

At roughly 48–51× forward earnings on current-year estimates and 20–22× sales, a modest miss can overwhelm strong long-term economics. The shares fell 13–14% after the Q1 double miss.

Medium · structural

Payment for order flow

No specific 2026 SEC ban was verified. The risk remains structural: a significant policy change would impair a major revenue stream, and the absence of a new action is not proof of permanent policy stability.

Medium · execution

Product and reputational risk

AI-directed trading, leverage and perpetuals can create regulatory or reputational damage if retail losses become prominent. Promotional fee waivers may also add cost before newer products contribute revenue.

Market · elevated

High-beta exposure

A 2.47 beta amplifies market shocks. Insider 10b5-1 sales may weigh on sentiment, while competition from Schwab, Interactive Brokers and Fidelity continues to improve.

Repurchase capacity

The March 2026 authorization replaced prior programs, added roughly $1.1 billion of incremental capacity, has no expiration and targets execution over approximately three years. Management can accelerate on a perceived dislocation between price and value.

Liquidity and funding

Robinhood ended 2025 with $4.332 billion of cash and equivalents and $8.695 billion including segregated and restricted cash. It later issued $2 billion of convertible notes, earmarking $300 million for repurchases, and arranged a $3.25 billion 364-day revolver for Robinhood Securities.

Capital-allocation interpretation

Repurchases are economically attractive only below intrinsic value. Morningstar's $50 fair value and the desk's $130 target frame an unusually wide range; that disagreement makes buyback execution price—not authorization size—the key variable.
