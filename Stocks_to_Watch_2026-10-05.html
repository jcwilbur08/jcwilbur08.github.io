Hold ratings apply only where evidence supports an actual view; conviction is 1–10. Forward P/E and PEG are unavailable where a current reliable consensus data source could not be verified. Scenario probabilities are judgmental. Workbook weights are static source fields; no MTD/YTD performance is calculated.
