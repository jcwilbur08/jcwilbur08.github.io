03 · Business quality, industry structure & moat

Core underwriting pillars

  • Critical supplier to NVIDIA, Apple, AMD and hyperscaler silicon designs; multi-node tape-out ecosystem and yield learning are hard to replicate.
  • N2 ramp and CoWoS/advanced packaging produce high-value wafer and packaging mix that should support long-duration operating margins if capacity stays scarce.
  • Price increases, design scale and disciplined utilization can offset some geographic expansion costs.
  • Net cash provides flexibility through a large capital cycle, but not all cash can be treated as available for shareholder distribution.

Strongest counterevidence and competitive pressure

  • Overseas fab economics can dilute consolidated gross margins and lock in years of capex before acceptable returns are observable.
  • AI customer concentration and cloud-provider internal silicon decisions can create correlated order revisions; N2 capacity is planned into an uncertain 2028 demand curve.
  • Taiwan geopolitical constraints, trade controls and cross-strait transport risk are not captured by a normal equity discount rate.
  • At ~$420, a large amount of future operating success is already recognized despite substantial near-term capex.
A strong business can be a poor security when the entry price demands an unrealistically good path—or a high-return idea can fail because operating economics change. The contrary cases above are treated as alternative hypotheses to test, not as fine-print risks.

09 · Catalysts, dated checkpoints & thesis-break criteria

Evidence to demand next

  • Q4 revenue $52.3–54.3B; gross margin 65–67% and operating margin 56.5–58.5%.
  • 2027 N2-family more-than-doubling capacity plan, yields and cash returns; monthly revenue and HPC demand.
  • Incremental CoWoS/advanced-packaging capacity monetization and AI customer order diversification.
  • 2027–28 U.S. fab and potential Texas commitments: firm contracts, pricing, incentives and utilization.

Explicit thesis-break conditions

  • Two quarters of meaningful advanced-node capacity underutilization or falling AI/HPC demand without clear temporary causes.
  • N2 yield, delivery, or qualification setbacks materially prevent anticipated node economics and customer migration.
  • Structural gross margin below ~56% with sustained adverse returns from overseas construction.
  • Capex remains $60B+ as sales and operating cash flows materially weaken, depressing multi-year owner FCF.
  • Geopolitical conditions meaningfully interrupt Taiwan production, exports, customers or financial settlement.
Separation of roles: Weakness in forward valuation alone may trigger a suggested lower purchase price or watch posture without implying the underlying company moat broke. Breaking an operating condition triggers formal CIO review; this report does not alter the official status or current position.

Outstanding diligence questions

  • Are 2027/28 overseas-fab incentives effectively lowering the required investment per incremental wafer?
  • What proportion of current N2 and CoWoS capacity is contractually or commercially committed?
  • Will NT$/US$ exchange rates continue supporting dollar-denominated valuation assumptions?
