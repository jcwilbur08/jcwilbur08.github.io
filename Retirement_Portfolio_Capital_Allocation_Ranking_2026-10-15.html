Executive decision

The pullback now has an explanation, and it is about rates and financing, not fundamentals. Two straight sessions of semiconductor and AI-infrastructure weakness followed a hotter-than-expected September CPI (3.7% y/y vs. 3.6% expected; core 2.6% vs. 2.5%) on Oct. 14 and a 6.3% y/y PPI print (vs. 5.5% expected) on Oct. 15. Together they pushed the odds of a rate hike at the late-October FOMC to roughly a coin flip. Fresh worries about how AI capex is being financed and an overnight Asian memory-chip rout added to the selling. The two AI-supply-chain names that reported this week, ASML and TSM, both beat and raised guidance, and both traded lower. For a growth mandate backed by an $80,000 pension floor, macro-driven weakness in quality franchises is the setup for staged buying. Staged buying stops at the concentration limits, and the PM's Oct. 14 adds have already pushed against them.
