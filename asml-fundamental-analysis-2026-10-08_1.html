01 · Investment thesis

A superior asset with a thinner margin for error.

ASML remains the essential production bottleneck for advanced semiconductors. The operating evidence is strengthening—Q2 beat, 2026 guidance rose again, and 2027 EUV capacity is virtually spoken for. The reservation is price: roughly 42× forward earnings and a 2-star Morningstar rating leave the stock sensitive to any order, margin, or export-control disappointment.

Demand visibility

Customer commitments and down payments have effectively filled 2027 EUV capacity; TSMC and memory demand add independent confirmation.

Pricing power

Q2 gross margin reached 54.0%; Q3 guidance steps up to 55–57% as system mix and installed-base economics strengthen.

Platform depth

Low-NA EUV, High-NA EUV, immersion DUV and service revenue make ASML more than a single-node equipment story.

Portfolio verdict

Maintain Buy, 6/10. The 0.73% position is not excessive, but the pre-earnings setup argues against chasing. The street’s $1,970.33 average target implies 11.3% price upside from the supplied reference price—enough for Buy under the standing scale, but not enough to absorb a weak print cleanly.

The best near-term decision is to hold through the evidence, not front-run it.

03 · Demand & technology

The backlog is becoming capacity planning.

Low-NA EUV

ASML plans to move annual Low-NA EUV capacity from about 65 systems to roughly 85 in 2027 and potentially 110 in 2028. Morningstar reports the new 2027 capacity is virtually all booked with commitments and down payments, while the 2028 expansion is increasingly likely.

DUV immersion

Planned immersion capacity rises from about 130 systems to 170 in 2027 and potentially 220 in 2028. This matters because mature-node and China exposure still run through DUV, while advanced fabs also need DUV layers around EUV steps.

High-NA EUV

Intel is using High-NA in high-volume manufacturing on select 18A layers. ASML expanded collaboration with Intel, Samsung and TSMC; Samsung targets high-volume DRAM use by 2028. No new 2026 customer beyond those three was verified.

Memory

Memory represented 49% of Q2 net system sales. SK Hynix and Samsung commitments, plus strong AI-memory demand, support lithography intensity—but the customer set remains concentrated.

TSMC read-through: strong, not perfect

September revenue reached NT$511.86 billion, up 54.6% YoY. Q3 revenue of roughly NT$1.49 trillion beat the NT$1.46 trillion LSEG SmartEstimate and TSMC’s own guidance. That validates advanced-node and HPC demand into ASML’s print, but the monthly release does not disclose node or customer mix.

04 · China & controls

The revenue mix is normalizing before policy is settled.

China represented 33% of 2025 sales; management guided to roughly 20% for 2026. That decline is already part of the operating plan. The harder-to-price risk is servicing: installed DUV tools are economically valuable only when uptime and parts access remain intact.

What is in force

Dutch licensing applies to NXT:2000i-and-newer immersion tools since September 2023 and to NXT:1980i since September 2024, with case-by-case reviews. China’s accumulated tool base keeps service revenue relevant.

What is not yet law

The bipartisan MATCH Act would restrict immersion DUV sales and require licenses for servicing named Chinese customers. A House markup passed 44–0, but the Senate companion remained in committee as of September 9. No new September–October rule was verified.

05 · Valuation

Wide moat, high uncertainty, premium price.

At the cited market snapshot, ASML traded at 57.07× trailing and 41.67× forward earnings, with a 0.94 PEG ratio and 1.77 beta. Morningstar assigns a Wide Moat, High Uncertainty, €1,800 fair value estimate and 2-star rating. The same research projects roughly €70 billion of revenue and €69 of EPS in 2028.

The valuation can work

ASML has the scarce asset, evidence of margin expansion and a visible capacity ramp. Consensus FY EPS rises from about $44 in 2026 to $58.49 in 2027, a 32.9% increase. If that growth arrives, today’s multiple compresses quickly without a price decline.

The valuation can break

A roughly 42× forward multiple magnifies any miss. The risk is less a demand collapse than a timing gap—orders, shipments or gross margin arriving one or two quarters later than the valuation assumes.

07 · Risk register

What can invalidate the call.

High impact

Export controls broaden from sales to service

A servicing restriction would reach the installed tool base, not just incremental shipments. Policy is not enacted, but scenario estimates show a double-digit earnings sensitivity.

High impact

AI capital spending digests earlier than expected

The bull case assumes multi-year AI infrastructure intensity. A pause would propagate from leading-edge logic and HBM into lithography planning while the stock still carries a premium multiple.

Medium–high

Customer concentration turns into timing risk

TSMC, Samsung and Intel anchor ASML’s opportunity set. A pushout by one customer can create quarterly volatility even if long-run demand remains intact.

Medium–high

High-NA ramp misses productivity or utilization goals

High-NA is both a technology transition and a capacity ramp. Shipment slippage or weak utilization could delay revenue and mix benefits.

Medium

Valuation de-rates without an earnings miss

At roughly 42× forward earnings, higher rates or a rotation away from AI capital-equipment beneficiaries can compress the multiple even if estimates hold.
