01 · Investment call

Maintain the target. Raise no new gate.

Strong Buy, 8/10, with the $545 twelve-month target maintained. The operating evidence strengthened: Q3 beat, Q4 guidance stepped up, full-year growth reached roughly 44%, and management framed capacity spending against identifiable demand. The target already anticipated this trajectory, so the disciplined response is to maintain—not chase.

Why conviction stays at 8

Demand commentary was exceptional, N2 adoption accelerated, leading-edge mix reached 79% of wafer revenue, and high-performance computing represented 68% of the platform mix. But 2026 has now produced three record-beat selloffs. The market is withholding near-term credit while 2027 capital intensity remains unresolved.

Reference price is the $430.00 Wednesday, October 14 close. Thursday premarket indications were lower, roughly $418–425, while the session was still in progress.

Desk verdict

Nothing in the print weakens the thesis. The current drawdown reflects macro and sector pressure plus concern about the cost of serving AI demand—not a shortfall in demand, margins, or guidance.

03 · Guidance & preview bar

Every substantive hurdle cleared

Q4 revenue guidance of $52.3–54.3B implies roughly 14% sequential growth at the midpoint. Gross margin is guided to 65–67% and operating margin to 56.5–58.5%. Full-year USD revenue growth moved to roughly 44%, the third raise this year.

The unresolved number

Management said 2026–2028 capex will be significantly higher than the prior three years but supplied no official 2027 figure. External estimates of $83–90B define the overhang; they are analyst forecasts, not company guidance.

04 · Demand, nodes & capacity

The constraint is still supply

“Demand is extremely robust. We did not see any sign of slowdown.”C.C. Wei · Q3 2026 earnings call

N2 inflects

2nm rose to 8% of wafer revenue from 3% in Q2. Management expects annual N2-family capacity to more than double in 2027.

HPC dominates

High-performance computing reached 68% of revenue. Advanced nodes—7nm and below—accounted for 79% of wafer revenue.

Packaging scales

CoWoS is in production at five-times reticle size, with nine-times and fourteen-times designs in development. No explicit new lead-time number was disclosed.

Capacity without apology

Management described a still-large supply gap, said it had no intention to leave demand for others, and linked spending to customer roadmaps. Arizona Fab 2 is now expected to enter volume production in Q3 2027, ahead of the prior schedule.

The prudence clause

Management remains careful about 2027 planning because consumer and price-sensitive markets face component inflation and macro uncertainty. Overseas fabs dilute gross margin by roughly two points today, widening to three to four points over the next several years.

05 · Market reaction

A record beat sold into a risk-off tape

The ADR closed Wednesday at $430.00 and indicated roughly $418–425 before Thursday's open. Taipei shares had already closed 2% lower as the Taiex fell 3.41%. The reaction extends the 2026 pattern: all three record beats have been sold, even when the operating result cleared expectations.

What drove the move

The quarter itself was not the problem. Hot September inflation, a Korean chip selloff, and renewed concern about the capital required to fund AI infrastructure overwhelmed the beat-and-raise. Samsung fell 5.29% and SK Hynix 8.36% in the regional rout.

Interpretation

The selloff lowers the entry price but does not eliminate the capital-intensity debate. The market is demanding proof that extraordinary spending converts into durable free cash flow and sustained margins.

07 · Risks & open questions

The thesis is stronger. The debate is not finished.

Primary overhang

2027 capex intensity

No company figure was provided. External estimates at $83–90B imply a step-up large enough to pressure free cash flow and keep the valuation debate active.

Margin risk

N2 and overseas dilution

The N2 ramp reduced sequential gross margin, while overseas fabs are expected to widen their current roughly two-point drag to three to four points over several years.

End-market risk

AI strength masks softer segments

Consumer and price-sensitive categories remain exposed to component inflation and macro uncertainty, which management explicitly flagged in its 2027 planning.

Still open

China, pricing and CoWoS timing

No China-specific call commentary, explicit new pricing figure, or new CoWoS lead-time number was verified. Those gaps remain open rather than filled by inference.

Execution risk

The tape may stay hostile

Three 2026 record beats have now sold off. Near-term multiple expansion cannot be assumed even if the earnings path remains intact.
