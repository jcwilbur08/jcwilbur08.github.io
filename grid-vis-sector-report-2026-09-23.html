3. GRID — Deep Dive

What it is

GRID tracks the Nasdaq Clean Edge Smart Grid Infrastructure Index (123 holdings). "Smart grid" per the index methodology covers four eligible activities: (1) electric grid; (2) electric meters, devices and networks; (3) energy storage and EV network management; (4) enabling software for the grid. Companies are classified Pure Play (≥50% of revenue from these activities — collectively 80% of index weight, 8% single-name cap) or Diversified (20% weight). Reconstituted semi-annually. This is not a utilities fund — it's the equipment, engineering, and software layer of electrification.

4. VIS — Deep Dive

What it is

VIS tracks the MSCI US Investable Market Industrials 25/50 Index — 396 stocks spanning large-, mid-, and small-cap U.S. industrials under GICS, with RIC-style diversification caps (no name >25%; names >5% capped at 50% combined). Fee: 0.09%. AUM $8.5B ETF class ($9.1B total fund). This is the cheapest way to own the entire U.S. industrial economy in one ticker.

6. Demand Drivers — Why This Corner Still Works

  • Grid modernization supercycle: U.S. transmission investment is in a multi-year upcycle; Quanta's backlog and Eaton's electrical orders are the hard data. GRID owns both.
  • Data-center power demand: your GEV report documented >$5B YTD data-center electrification orders — 2× all of 2025. Every GW of data-center load is transformers, switchgear, and cable: GRID's top 5.
  • Reshoring & manufacturing construction: CAT, Deere, Parker-Hannifin, Fastenal — the VIS/PAVE holdings — ride domestic industrial capex directly.
  • Defense: 22.8% of VIS is aerospace & defense (RTX, GE Aerospace, Boeing) — a durable budget-backed leg most "industrials" tourists miss.
  • Valuation: industrials broadly trade at reasonable multiples vs. the 2-star mega-cap tech you're trimming — this sleeve is the rotation destination, not the funding source.

7. Recommendation for Your Book

GRID — BUY / accumulate on weakness. Keep the 60 shares; add 15–20 shares (~$2.7–3.6K) if it retests the Q3 lows. This is your highest-conviction thematic sleeve: 5-star, $12B AUM, pure electrification exposure you can't replicate with single stocks at this weight.

VIS — HOLD, but decide. 16 shares ($5.2K) is a stub, not a position. Two clean options:
A) Fold into GRID (~$5.2K → ~29 more GRID shares, taking GRID to ~$16K / 0.95%) — simplest, concentrates your highest-conviction theme, and removes the GEV/Uber/Eaton overlap duplication.
B) Build into a $12–15K broad-industrials core (+20–30 shares) — keeps the 9bp all-cap beta as ballast alongside GRID's theme.
My recommendation is A: your direct GEV and UBER holdings already give you the large-cap industrial exposure VIS would add. Don't pay for overlap.

PAVE — watchlist. If domestic infrastructure-capex momentum continues, a 1–1.5% PAVE position is the logical third leg. Not urgent with GRID covering electrification.
