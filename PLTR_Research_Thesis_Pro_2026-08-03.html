Bottom line

Palantir is operating like a category-defining AI platform. Q2 2026 revenue grew 93%, U.S. commercial revenue grew 149%, U.S. government revenue grew 90%, net dollar retention reached 157%, and total remaining deal value rose 83% to $13.1 billion. The company also produced a 47% GAAP operating margin and more than $1.2 billion of free cash flow in a single quarter.

The issue is not the business; it is the price. At $144.42, fully diluted equity value is approximately $371 billion and enterprise value approximately $362 billion. That implies 44.4x management's FY2026 revenue guidance and 108x trailing free cash flow. Even a base case requiring 55% growth in 2027, 40% growth in 2028, and a 24x 2028 sales multiple produces a present value near $128 per share.

Research posture: Maintain on the watchlist. A more attractive initial research entry zone is $105-$120, with a staged framework rather than a full position. The business can outperform this model; the stock needs that outperformance merely to earn a normal premium return.

What is working

  • U.S. commercial adoption is accelerating, not decelerating.
  • Government growth is broad-based across defense and civil programs.
  • AIP, Ontology, and forward-deployed engineering create a deployment moat.
  • Revenue growth and margins are improving simultaneously.
  • $9.2B net-cash-like liquidity supports resilience and optionality.
  • RPO, remaining deal value, bookings, and NDR support forward visibility.
  • Commercial revenue is becoming a larger and faster-growing engine.
  • GAAP profitability reduces reliance on adjusted metrics.

What is weakening or unresolved

  • International commercial revenue grew only 26% and 2% sequentially.
  • Stock-based compensation remains meaningful at about 14% of TTM revenue.
  • Founder voting control materially limits outside shareholder influence.
  • Valuation leaves little room for growth normalization or multiple compression.
  • U.S. concentration is increasing: 81%+ of Q2 revenue came from the U.S.
  • Q2 EPS included a $0.03 GAAP benefit from unrealized SpaceX gains.

Bull view

Palantir becomes the standard control plane for enterprise and government AI. AIP accelerates deployments, Ontology embeds workflows, and customers scale contracts rapidly. Growth remains above 50% through 2028 with 50%+ FCF margins.

Base view

U.S. strength persists but growth moderates from exceptional levels. International adoption improves gradually. Margins remain elite, yet valuation compresses as the company matures. Business wins; stock returns are more modest.

Bear view

Growth normalizes faster, competition increases, government timing creates volatility, and international commercial remains slow. Even strong fundamentals cannot offset severe multiple compression from an extreme starting valuation.

Variant perception

Consensus increasingly accepts Palantir's AI leadership. The differentiated view is therefore no longer "Palantir can grow." The real variant must be that Palantir can sustain both hypergrowth and extraordinary margins for longer than the market's already aggressive expectations. Without that duration, valuation becomes the downside mechanism.

What appears priced in

  • FY2026 revenue near the raised $8.15B-$8.158B range and continued quarterly beats.
  • U.S. commercial growth that remains well above normal enterprise software levels.
  • Durable 40%+ GAAP operating margins and 50%+ adjusted free cash flow margins.
  • A persistent premium to all large-cap software and defense technology peers.
  • Limited dilution and no major policy, procurement, privacy, or competitive disruption.

Why a DCF is unusually fragile here

  • Small changes in 2027-2030 growth rates produce large valuation swings.
  • Terminal multiples remain a dominant driver because current margins and growth are far above steady-state software norms.
  • Dilution, tax normalization, and SBC economics matter even when headline free cash flow is high.
  • The current price effectively capitalizes future success years before it is earned.

Valuation interpretation

At the late after-hours price, the market is paying a higher multiple on FY2026 revenue than this model's base-case 2028 exit multiple. That does not make the stock uninvestable, but it means the return thesis depends on sustained upside revisions rather than simple execution against current guidance.

Valuation discipline: Avoid chasing the post-earnings gap. A starter position is justified only as a deliberately small option on long-run category dominance, not because the current price offers an obvious margin of safety.

Governance assessment

Palantir's Class F and multi-class structure gives founders effective control over major stockholder matters for the foreseeable future. This can protect long-term strategy, but outside investors have limited ability to influence board composition, compensation, mergers, or other major actions. The governance discount should not disappear simply because operating results are strong.

Accounting and earnings-quality cautions

  • Adjusted metrics exclude stock-based compensation and related payroll taxes; the cost is noncash but economically dilutive.
  • Q2 GAAP EPS benefited from unrealized investment gains, so operating income and cash flow are better core measures.
  • Government deal value can include options and contracts with termination-for-convenience clauses; total contract value is not identical to guaranteed revenue.
  • Low tax rates may normalize as tax benefits and stock-compensation effects evolve.

Recommendation and Action Framework

Watch
Conviction: Medium. Palantir is one of the strongest fundamental growth stories in public software, but the late after-hours valuation offers inadequate base-case asymmetry for a new position.

Recommendation rationale

  1. Fundamentals merit attention. The combination of 93% growth, 47% GAAP operating margin, 62% adjusted operating margin, and 157% NDR is extraordinary.
  2. The thesis is increasingly validated. Commercial AI deployments and government mission programs are scaling together, reducing the old concern that Palantir was only a niche contractor.
  3. Valuation is the gating factor. A fully diluted post-earnings EV around $362B demands sustained hypergrowth and premium terminal multiples.
  4. Portfolio overlap matters. The existing portfolio already has roughly 30.3% selected tech/digital exposure and 28.1% AI-linked exposure.
  5. Best implementation is staged. Research starter only at a better price, then add upon operating confirmation rather than chasing a single earnings gap.

Upgrade to Add

  • Price below $120 with thesis intact.
  • Q3 revenue exceeds guide and U.S. commercial remains above 100% growth.
  • FY2027 guide supports 50%+ growth.
  • SBC/revenue trends toward 12% or below.

Remain Watch

  • Price remains above $130-$140.
  • Execution is excellent but already reflected in estimates.
  • International commercial growth remains modest.
  • Scenario-weighted return remains near or below hurdle rate.

Avoid / downgrade

  • U.S. commercial growth drops below 65% for two quarters.
  • NDR falls below 125%.
  • Material government program loss or security incident.
  • Share dilution or SBC reaccelerates.
