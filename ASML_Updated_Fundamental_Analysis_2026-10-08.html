Executive View

ASML remains one of the highest-quality businesses in public markets. Its EUV monopoly, installed-base service economics, supplier ecosystem, and customer dependence on its roadmap are intact. The long-term thesis strengthened modestly since the September 22 underwriting because Samsung, TSMC, SK Hynix, and Intel have provided clearer evidence of High-NA adoption and because ASML and customers are working on a larger-mask platform that could improve High-NA productivity.

The stock, however, has moved faster than the earnings estimates. The ADR closed at $1,769.00 on October 8, up about 3.4% from the $1,711.32 reference in the prior report. Current 2026 consensus remains approximately EUR 42.9 billion of revenue and EUR 38.4-38.7 of EPS. The result is a good company at a fair-to-rich price, with the next major information event only six days away.

Portfolio verdict: Hold existing shares. Add only in stages and preferably after either a pullback or confirmation from the October 14 report. Business Model, Moat, and Competitive Position

ASML sells lithography systems that pattern semiconductor wafers and then monetizes the installed base through service, field options, upgrades, and productivity improvements. EUV is the strategic center. It is required for the most advanced logic nodes and is increasingly relevant to advanced memory roadmaps. DUV, metrology and inspection, computational lithography, and installed-base management provide breadth beyond the EUV headline.

  • EUV monopoly: ASML is the only commercial supplier of EUV lithography systems. Nikon and Canon compete in older DUV categories, but do not offer a commercial EUV substitute.
  • Customer lock-in: Foundries and memory manufacturers plan node roadmaps, fab layouts, process recipes, and yield learning around ASML systems. Switching is not a normal procurement decision.
  • Supplier ecosystem: Carl Zeiss optics, precision mechanics, light sources, software, and process integration create a system-level moat that is difficult to reproduce.
  • Installed-base compounding: 2025 service and field-option sales reached EUR 8.2 billion, up 26.2% year over year, creating a higher-quality revenue stream tied to a growing tool base.
  • High-NA extension: Samsung, TSMC, SK Hynix, and Intel commitments support the idea that ASML is extending its leadership rather than merely harvesting the current EUV generation.
  • Market-share context: Reuters cited a 94% global lithography share estimate for 2025. Exact share depends on category definition, but the relevant point is that ASML has no commercial rival in EUV.

Capital allocation is supportive rather than the main thesis. ASML combines dividends and share repurchases with high R&D investment. Risks, Red Flags, and Thesis Falsifiers

  • Export controls and China: China represented 29.1% of 2025 sales. ASML expects China to be about 20% of 2026 sales, but broader DUV restrictions could affect systems, service, and long-term customer relationships.
  • Customer concentration: the largest customer represented 23.9% of 2025 sales and the two largest represented 38.0%. Taiwan and South Korea represented 25.5% and 25.0% of 2025 sales, respectively.
  • AI capex cyclicality: the company can be fully booked and still be vulnerable to a change in customer returns, funding, or utilization assumptions.
  • High-NA economics: the technology is strategically important, but $400 million-class systems, throughput, yield, mask constraints, and cost per wafer still need to prove out at scale.
  • Capacity execution: 30% planned output increases increase dependence on suppliers, skilled labor, installation capacity, and working-capital discipline.
  • Valuation: a premium multiple magnifies downside when bookings or 2027 estimates roll over. The company can remain excellent while the stock underperforms.

Conclusion

ASML's fundamental thesis is stronger than it was in September, but the stock is also less forgiving. High-NA customer commitments and the larger-mask initiative support a durable multi-generation moat. The core debate has moved from “is ASML still indispensable?” to “how much of the 2027-2028 earnings curve is already capitalized?”

Final recommendation: Hold / add on weakness, conviction 8.0 out of 10. Keep the 7-share position. Do not chase a large add at $1,769 immediately before the October 14 report. A small add is acceptable only if you are deliberately building toward about 1.0% and are comfortable with the event risk. The preferred accumulation zone is below $1,700, the better risk/reward zone is below $1,550, and the thesis should be re-underwritten before any aggressive purchase below $1,450.

Decision hinge: ASML must convert its 2026 demand reset into a durable 2027-2028 earnings base. What must be true: EUV demand stays sold out through 2027, High-NA adoption continues, gross margins remain in the mid-50s, China risk stays manageable, and cash conversion remains strong. Research posture: Hold existing shares; staged Add on weakness.
