Observed facts vs interpretation

The Fed hike raises the hurdle; it does not invalidate the growth thesis

Observed facts: The Fed raised rates 25 bps to 3.75%–4.00%; its 2026 median policy-rate projection is 4.1%; the 10-year Treasury ended around 5%; Brent closed at $104.87; the S&P 500 slipped 0.1% for the week while the Nasdaq gained 0.7% and the Dow lost 1.7%.

Research conclusion: The market is rewarding near-term earnings durability but demanding more evidence for long-duration valuation. That favors businesses with visible cash conversion and pricing power, and it penalizes rate-sensitive securities whose value depends primarily on lower discount rates.
