Highest-priority opportunities

1. UBER - best tactical asymmetry, staging on plan

The Sep. 19 re-underwrite's ~$90 probability-weighted value (bear $55 / base $93 / bull $120) implies roughly 26% upside from today's $71.32. The position grew from 70 to 76 shares in the new workbook — exactly the staged-accumulation plan at work. AV capital intensity remains the risk to watch.

2. GEHC - undervaluation thesis, narrowing margin of safety

At $68.79, GEHC is up 7.62% since Oct. 8 — the largest move within the priority tier — while the share count held flat at 52. That's the right instinct (no chasing), but the underlying thesis has still never had a formal re-underwrite despite three consecutive reports flagging it. The gap between "priority" and "properly diligenced" is now wide enough to require a decision: write it up, or stop staging into it.

3. TSM - still preferred, entry improved

TSM retains the strongest blend of process leadership, AI/HPC demand, scale economics and valuation among the semiconductor choices here. The stock is 2.36% cheaper than Oct. 8, and the 26-to-33-share add landed into that better price — good execution. Taiwan geopolitical risk remains the non-diversifiable thesis risk.

4-6. VXUS / AVUV / AVDV - the stated priority that wasn't funded

All three remain top-of-list on portfolio-construction grounds: the book is still U.S.-heavy, and these are the cleanest diversifiers available. But VXUS and AVUV share counts are unchanged in the new workbook, and AVDV remains unfunded. If VXUS/AVUV gap closure is genuinely the first redeployment priority, this cycle's capital allocation didn't reflect that.

7. ADBE - initiated, the plan working as intended

A new 34-share (0.479%) position appeared in the Oct. 9 workbook, converting the Oct. 8 "selective initiation" call into an actual holding at an essentially unchanged price. The diligence question is unchanged: whether generative AI strengthens or erodes Creative Cloud / Acrobat seat monetization.

GEV continues to trade further above the Sep. 18 re-underwrite's own ~$935 base case and ~$951 scenario-weighted value, and was trimmed (not added to) in the new workbook — selling into strength rather than chasing is reasonable, though the rationale is worth confirming directly. GRID is essentially unchanged and still above the preferred add zone.

What could change the ranking quickly?

Move higher

  • NVDA below roughly $205 without a thesis break — the bar the recent add was supposed to wait for.
  • GEV back into roughly the $850-$875 zone with backlog and margin evidence intact.
  • GRID or further ASML pullbacks without estimate deterioration.
  • A VXUS/AVUV tranche actually getting funded, which would validate rather than just restate the stated priority.
  • Further evidence that UBER's AV capital commitments earn attractive returns through third-party financing and platform economics.

Move lower

  • 10-year yields reversing back above 5.25%+ for a sustained stretch with no offsetting earnings revisions.
  • UBER AV commitments accelerating while platform economics deteriorate.
  • GEHC PCS / FCF execution missing the current checkpoints — still unwritten, still overdue, now three reports running.
  • Further unreviewed adds to NVDA, ASML or other semiconductor names at prices above the stated preferred zones.
  • Additional trims like VIS or SOXQ without a documented rationale, which make the portfolio harder to reconcile against the research view over time.
