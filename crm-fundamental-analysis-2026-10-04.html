Executive Summary

Investment conclusion

Salesforce is the highest-quality large-cap software compounder the market is currently discounting. Agentforce has crossed $1.5B of ARR growing 240% year over year, combined Agentforce + Data 360 ARR is nearly $3.9B growing 210%+, current RPO is accelerating at +14%, and the business converts revenue into roughly $15B of annual free cash flow — yet the stock trades at about 14x forward earnings while the S&P 500 trades near 19x and direct peers ServiceNow and Oracle trade at 28x and 18x.

Research posture: Buy | Conviction 6/10 | 12-month target $285

At $234.69, CRM trades at roughly 18.4x FY2028E consensus non-GAAP EPS of ~$15.5 and 12.7x trailing free cash flow — an undemanding multiple for an 11%-growing business with 34%+ non-GAAP operating margins and a real, scaling AI franchise. The $285 target implies ~21% price upside and sits squarely inside the Street’s $275–$286 consensus cluster. Conviction is 6 rather than higher because the headline earnings are flattered by strategic-investment gains, full-year free-cash-flow growth guidance was cut to ~4–5%, and the company levered up $25B to fund the accelerated buyback. This is a quality-compounder Buy on a reasonable price — not a deep-value call, and not a momentum chase.

01 | Update

What changed since September 22

Twelve days, one flagship conference, one closed acquisition

Sep 10 — M&A closed

Fin acquisition completed ($3.6B, announced June). Intercom’s AI customer agent brings 30,000+ enterprise customers and a 76% average resolution rate across chat, email, WhatsApp, SMS, voice and Slack. It is now one of the seven named role-specific agents in the Dreamforce lineup (Fin: complex customer-experience workflows). One of the two $200M acquisition contributions to the FY2027 guide raise is now de-risked; Contentful ($1.5B) remains expected to close in Q3 FY2027.

Sep 15–17 — Dreamforce 2026

The most important product event since the Agentforce launch. Salesforce unveiled AIforce — an interface layer that routes Salesforce’s data, permissions and business logic into external AI environments (Claude, Slack, Amazon) via the Model Context Protocol, so workers never need to open Salesforce. Three launch surfaces: Claudeforce (prebuilt MCP server inside Anthropic’s Claude, 37 ready-made sales skills, beta, piloted by Deloitte, GitLab and Legora), Slackforce (Slackbot with Salesforce context, Slack CRM, Slack Code), and Agentforce Coworker (AI teammate inside Lightning; 100,000 activations in 35 days). Koa, the first purpose-built CRM reasoning model (built on NVIDIA Nemotron, trained on synthetic scenarios from 27 years of deployments), goes GA in winter 2026. Seven named agents — Casey, Paige, Carter, Marshall, Piper, Fin, Hunter (outbound sales, GA planned November). Benioff shared the stage with Anthropic’s Dario Amodei, NVIDIA’s Jensen Huang and OpenAI’s Sam Altman; Siemens confirmed Agentforce connected to Teamcenter. AWS partnership expanded (Bedrock model access, AWS Marketplace distribution). Zero data retention by model providers.

Sep 16 — Analyst action

Mizuho raised its target $265 → $280 (Outperform), citing the new interfaces unveiled at Dreamforce and calling the 2026 setup “much better” than 2025. Wells Fargo raised $230 → $250 (Equal Weight). Earlier in the window: Morgan Stanley $315 (Sep 2), Cantor Fitzgerald $250 → $300 Overweight (Sep 2), Citizens JMP $315 Market Outperform (Sep 18), DA Davidson $225 (Sep 17), Needham Buy reaffirmed (Sep 17), Jefferies Buy reaffirmed (Sep 24).

Sep 16 — Outage

A widespread service disruption hit core Salesforce services across multiple regions during Dreamforce’s second day — severe delays, intermittent errors, some services inaccessible — while the company was on stage pitching platform reliability. The timing was embarrassing and bears watching, but there is no evidence of lasting customer or financial impact.

Sep 22 → Oct 2 — Price action

Stock drifted -8.8% from the post-Q2 high of $251.61 to $234.69, giving back part of the +22.6% earnings-day pop on no company-specific negative news — a sentiment fade, not a fundamental deterioration. The 52-week range is $146.32–$269.11; the stock sits ~13% below its high and is down ~11% YTD.

Net assessment. The last twelve days were modestly positive on fundamentals: Dreamforce materially strengthened the AI narrative (the interface-threat answer is now a shipped product, not a slide), Fin closed, and the analyst community moved targets up. The outage and the price fade are noise against that. Nothing in the window changes the earnings-quality or leverage caveats — they remain the binding constraints on conviction.
02 | Business & Industry

Business quality and competitive position

Salesforce is the largest enterprise CRM software vendor; subscriptions and support are ~95% of revenue, plus professional services. In Q3 FY2026 the company renamed its subscription lines to reference Agentforce with no change to revenue allocation: Agentforce Sales ($9.0B FY2026, +8%), Agentforce Service ($9.8B, +8%), Agentforce 360 Platform / Slack / Other ($8.9B, +23%, includes Informatica), Agentforce Marketing & Commerce ($5.4B, +3%), Agentforce Integration & Analytics ($6.2B, +8%).

Competitive position

Advantage

System-of-record footprint

The CRM installed base is the distribution surface for every AI product — Agentforce, Slackbot and Coworker all monetize on top of data and workflows customers already run on Salesforce.

Advantage

Data gravity

Data 360 ingested 104 trillion records in Q2 (+355%), 82 trillion via Zero Copy (+731%). Models commoditize; the proprietary customer-data estate does not.

Contested

vs. Microsoft Dynamics

Microsoft competes on bundled Copilot/AI pricing inside enterprise agreements. Salesforce’s answer is usage-based AWU monetization and now the AIforce interface layer — be the data and permissions layer every assistant must call.

Contested

vs. ServiceNow / hyperscalers

ServiceNow (27.8x forward) and the hyperscalers compete on workflow and data platforms. Salesforce’s edge is the CRM system of record plus Slack distribution; Zero Copy is the architectural answer to native cloud data stacks.

The interface threat, answered. The existential question for every application vendor — what happens when employees open an AI assistant instead of the app? — got a shipped answer at Dreamforce: AIforce puts Salesforce’s governed data, permissions and business logic inside Claude, Slack and other surfaces via MCP. The moat thesis shifts from “owns the screen” to “owns the governed data and workflow layer every screen must call.”

Earnings-quality filter — read this before the headline. Q2’s $5.90 non-GAAP EPS included $2.61B of net gains on strategic investments (~$2.43/share) versus $6M a year earlier. Medium

Cash-flow guide cut

FY2027 OCF/FCF growth of ~4–5% vs. the earlier ~9–10% framework. Timing or structural? The December 2 print must answer.

Medium

Acquired growth

~3+ points of guided growth is Informatica; organic is ~8%. Contentful still pending; $200M of the FY2027 raise depends on the two H2 closings.

Medium

Seat-based pricing vs. AI automation

AWUs are the strategic answer, but most sales/service customers have not upgraded to premium AI editions — seat compression risk is live until conversion proves out.

Watch

Platform reliability

The Dreamforce-week outage was embarrassing; no lasting impact is evident, but recurrence would damage the trust narrative AIforce depends on.
