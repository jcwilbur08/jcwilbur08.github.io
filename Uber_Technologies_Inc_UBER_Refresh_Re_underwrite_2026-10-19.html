01 · Executive investment judgment

At approximately 14× trailing free cash flow, Uber still offers attractive normalized upside—provided management protects marketplace returns and does not overinvest in autonomous fleets.

Business economics

  • Dominant multi-sided rider/driver/merchant matching raises utilization and product cross-sell while the core platform remains mostly asset-light.
  • Mobility and Delivery booking scale supports higher incremental margins, Uber One subscriber attachment and advertising take rates.
  • Autonomous-vehicle partners can use Uber as a demand and dispatch layer, preserving consumer acquisition advantage even if fleet ownership shifts.

Principal bear counterargument

  • AV operators may vertically integrate consumer distribution, weaken Uber pricing power, or force Uber to finance vehicles and depots.
  • Delivery Hero increases capital needs, execution complexity and exposure to multiple regulatory regimes.
  • Driver classification, minimum earnings and safety requirements can make existing marketplace margins structurally unsustainable.

03 · Business quality, industry structure & moat

Core underwriting pillars

  • Dominant multi-sided rider/driver/merchant matching raises utilization and product cross-sell while the core platform remains mostly asset-light.
  • Mobility and Delivery booking scale supports higher incremental margins, Uber One subscriber attachment and advertising take rates.
  • Autonomous-vehicle partners can use Uber as a demand and dispatch layer, preserving consumer acquisition advantage even if fleet ownership shifts.
  • Share repurchases and disciplined corporate overhead can convert strong FCF into faster growth in cash flow per share.

Strongest counterevidence and competitive pressure

  • AV operators may vertically integrate consumer distribution, weaken Uber pricing power, or force Uber to finance vehicles and depots.
  • Delivery Hero increases capital needs, execution complexity and exposure to multiple regulatory regimes.
  • Driver classification, minimum earnings and safety requirements can make existing marketplace margins structurally unsustainable.
  • Investment revaluations inflate reported GAAP EPS; reported free cash flow still requires maintenance-normalization, debt-service and acquisition consideration.
A strong business can be a poor security when the entry price demands an unrealistically good path—or a high-return idea can fail because operating economics change. The contrary cases above are treated as alternative hypotheses to test, not as fine-print risks.

09 · Catalysts, dated checkpoints & thesis-break criteria

Evidence to demand next

  • November 4 Q3 booking growth 18–22% constant currency and non-GAAP EPS $0.84–0.88.
  • Mobility and Delivery non-GAAP operating margins: sequential gains, price/take-rate discipline and advertising contribution.
  • Delivery Hero regulatory and financing milestones; target sub-2x gross leverage and integration economics.
  • AV commercialization across partners: fleet funding, utilization, partner bypass and per-vehicle trip economics.

Explicit thesis-break conditions

  • Two consecutive quarters with constant-currency bookings growth below 15%, excluding exceptional external disruption.
  • Normalized trailing FCF falls below $8B because of recurring operating deterioration.
  • Share count starts rising materially despite repurchases; SBC and acquisition dilution outgrow per-share economics.
  • Autonomous partnerships bypass Uber or require fleet ownership that makes returns unattractive.
  • Delivery Hero acquisition materially raises leverage or leads to persistent deterioration in segment profitability.
Separation of roles: Weakness in forward valuation alone may trigger a suggested lower purchase price or watch posture without implying the underlying company moat broke. Breaking an operating condition triggers formal CIO review; this report does not alter the official status or current position.

Outstanding diligence questions

  • Can fleet financing remain off Uber balance sheet without sacrificing economics, data ownership or customer access?
  • Is Delivery Hero’s acquisition price accretive on normalized FCF after financing and integration?
  • How much of Uber’s gross-bookings growth reflects share gains versus currency and existing-market penetration?
