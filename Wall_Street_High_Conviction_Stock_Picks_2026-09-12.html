Goldman Sachs and Bank of America

Goldman Sachs - September refresh

U.S.: Vertex Pharmaceuticals was added and Interactive Brokers removed, leaving 23 names. Goldman framed Vertex around as many as five potential multi-billion-dollar opportunities spanning cystic fibrosis, pain, kidney disease, hematology and endocrinology.

Europe: RWE, Adyen and Talanx were added; Enel, Wise, Hannover Re and Zalando were removed. Goldman sees RWE as evolving from a generator toward a more vertically integrated power platform and reportedly models stronger long-run earnings growth than consensus.

PM read: VRTX is the most important addition because it creates a second formal-bank endorsement after BofA's August US 1 inclusion. RWE is the most strategically interesting European addition given power scarcity, grid investment and data-center electrification.

Bank of America - US 1 rotation

BofA added Aramark and Clean Harbors to US 1 and removed Public Service Enterprise Group. US 1 is a best-ideas list drawn from BofA's U.S.-listed Buy-rated universe.

CLH: The fresh list placement is especially notable because William Blair separately initiated Clean Harbors at Outperform and added it to its own Conviction List this week. Earlier BofA coverage had highlighted Clean Harbors' leadership in hazardous-waste services.

ARMK: The fresh addition broadens the list into contracted food/facilities services, but public reporting around the list change contains less detailed thesis information than for CLH.

PM read: CLH deserves immediate fundamental work; ARMK belongs on the watchlist pending a clearer estimate/valuation wedge.

J.P. Morgan, Jefferies and Morgan Stanley

J.P. Morgan - Cogent Biosciences enters Analyst Focus

On September 9, JPMorgan analyst Anupam Rama added Cogent Biosciences to the Analyst Focus List while maintaining an Overweight rating and a $62 target. Public reporting says the thesis centers on bezuclastinib across multiple indications, with JPMorgan viewing the setup as favorable into potential late-2026/2027 approvals and initial launches.

Investor implication: COGT is a high-conviction research idea, but it is qualitatively different from MSFT or VRTX. The outcome distribution is wider, traditional earnings valuation is not yet useful, and position sizing would need to reflect clinical/regulatory binary risk.

Jefferies - Franchise Picks stable

No new U.S. Franchise Picks reshuffle was clearly verified in the September public sweep. Uber remains one of the more recent 2026 Franchise Picks, while the larger May refresh added names including Amazon, AppLovin, Credo, McDonald's, RadNet, Thermo Fisher and UL Solutions.

Investor implication: Do not confuse Jefferies' September India sector-pick publication with a U.S. Franchise Picks update. The formal U.S. list appears stable based on public information reviewed.

Morgan Stanley - Robinhood upgrade

Morgan Stanley upgraded Robinhood to Overweight from Equal-weight on September 1 and raised its target to $150 from $124. The firm expects broader product capabilities to improve assets, engagement and revenue captured per customer, and raised 2026-2028 earnings estimates.

Investor implication: This is a meaningful conviction increase, but not the same as a Research Analyst Top Pick designation. HOOD should be evaluated as a high-growth financial platform with substantial valuation and regulatory sensitivity.

Evercore, UBS, Citi and Barclays

Evercore ISI - Dell: thesis intact, tactical call harvested

Evercore removed Dell from its Tactical Outperform list after the stock's post-results rally but explicitly kept Dell as one of its Top Picks with an Outperform rating. Public analyst tracking subsequently showed a target increase to $650 on September 9.

Read-through: This is not a downgrade. It is a classic separation between long-term fundamental conviction and reduced short-term asymmetry after a strong move.

UBS - three fresh tactical Buys

UBS's September 7 Tactical Top Picks edition added Straumann Holding, Bristol-Myers Squibb and Barrick Mining, with reported targets of CHF 115, $80 and $50, respectively. The 12-name basket spans Europe and the Americas.

Read-through: Treat these as shorter-horizon ideas. UBS reported that 68 closed tactical ideas in 2026 had generated +0.4% average absolute performance but -1.1% average relative performance, so list status alone has not delivered excess returns YTD.

Citi - Babcock becomes European defense top pick

On September 11, Citi named Babcock International its top pick in European defense and cited roughly 60% upside to target. Citi argued that much of Babcock's U.K. defense exposure is tied to critical submarine and Royal Navy support, while Type 31 program risk should diminish as the first vessel nears completion.

Read-through: This is a valuation/derating recovery call rather than a pure revenue-momentum call, and offers differentiated geopolitical/defense exposure.

Barclays - bullish index call, no fresh stock list turnover verified

Barclays raised its 2026 S&P 500 target to 7,950 from 7,800 on September 9, citing strong earnings and AI-related investment, while flagging valuation, inflation and higher-rate risks. It also downgraded utilities to Neutral at the sector level.

Read-through: The macro backdrop remains supportive of earnings-driven equity exposure, but this should not be mistaken for a new named high-conviction stock list.

Cross-Bank Themes and PM Takeaways

1. Health care conviction is rising

VRTX is now on two formal best-idea lists, COGT joined JPMorgan's Analyst Focus List, and BMY entered UBS's tactical basket. This is the clearest new thematic shift versus the August report.

2. AI remains important, but the expression is broadening

MSFT remains a major cross-bank overlap; Dell remains an Evercore Top Pick. Yet fresh September adds are less concentrated in semiconductors, suggesting stock selection is broadening beyond the core AI complex.

3. Services and real-economy compounders are back in focus

BofA's ARMK and CLH additions push the best-ideas mix toward contracted services, hazardous waste, industrial activity and pricing power.

4. Defense/value trades remain active

Citi's Babcock call and UBS's Barrick addition show interest in geopolitical, commodity and value-sensitive exposures where rerating can matter as much as top-line growth.

Recommended diligence sequence

  1. VRTX: build a pipeline-weighted valuation that separates core CF cash flows from pain, kidney and endocrine optionality.
  2. CLH: test organic pricing, incineration capacity, EnviroServe integration, capital intensity and free-cash-flow conversion against the premium multiple.
  3. COGT: build approval/launch probability trees and downside cases before considering any position size.
  4. DELL: update AI server backlog conversion, gross-margin trajectory and valuation after the latest rally.
  5. MSFT: compare incremental AI revenue/margins with capex burden under a higher-for-longer rate environment.
  6. BMY / Babcock / ARMK: use as diversification candidates only after confirming the bank-specific earnings wedge and valuation support.
Overall research ranking: VRTX and CLH move to the top of the queue because their conviction signals are both fresh and independently reinforced. COGT is the most event-sensitive new idea; Dell and MSFT remain the strongest established large-cap research candidates; BMY, HOOD and ARMK are secondary follow-ups.
