  • Q3 (Oct 29): adj. EPS $7.20 (+45%), revenue $20.7B (+17%), adj. operating margin 20.6%, record backlog $78.1B. Power & Energy revenue +18%, segment profit +31%, driven partly by on-site data-center power.

PLTR — Palantir · $184.10 · Tier B

  • Valuation: ~$442B market cap at ~167x trailing GAAP earnings. Down 13.5% from its Oct 12 high; reports Nov 2 after the close.
  • What is priced in (sketch): a 10%/yr return for ten years needs ~$1.15T of value in 2036. At 30x earnings that is ~$38B of net income — several times today's level. This is a reverse-DCF sketch, not a model.
  • Preferred entry $130–150, aggressive ≤$120, no-add above $175. At $184 it is 23% above the band. Starter cap 0.5%.
  • Portfolio fit: AI software, so it correlates with the same capex narrative that drives a fifth of the book. The Nov 2 print and a write-up come before any decision.

  • Friday may be the start of a larger drawdown, not the end of one. The bands have aggressive levels for that reason; leave room for a second tranche.
  • It funds the diversifiers ~19% less than Option A.

For it

  • Each name is inside a band set before this week, by house research with stated scenarios — the entries were not chosen after the fact.
  • 2027 capex guidance from GOOG, AMZN and MSFT raises supplier revenue visibility even as it raises hyperscaler risk.
  • It executes most of the diversifier commitment in the same order, which the last five workbooks have not.
