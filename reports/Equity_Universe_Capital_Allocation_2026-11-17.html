DCF bridge

For a fully modeled operating company, enterprise value equals forecast after-tax unlevered cash flows discounted by WACC, plus terminal value; subtract net debt and other claims and divide by fully diluted shares. For buyback-heavy software firms, stock-based compensation must be treated as an economic owner cost, not ignored simply because it is noncash.

Example: Adobe's older $332 SBC-adjusted DCF compares to ~$237 spot, but weaker ARR conversion or multiple compression can overwhelm that spread. This is not a fresh November 17 audit. [S3]

Reverse DCF / implied hurdles

At the market price, solve for the growth/margin/terminal assumptions that equate DCF to that price; this makes expectations testable. The existing Adobe re-underwrite estimated roughly 1.2% annual economic FCFE growth under an explicitly illustrative 10-year case as of Nov 4; inputs and expected FCFE must be updated before reliance. For NVDA and TSM, 2027 EPS multiple comparisons do not replace cash-based valuation.

Formula: Price = [Σ FCFEₜ / (1+r)ᵗ + TV / (1+r)ᴺ] / diluted shares under an equity FCF approach. Reconcile tax, SBC, debt and reinvestment before use.

Scenario discipline

For the highest-ranked ideas, base requires credible revenue/earnings conversion and sustainable reinvestment; bear usually combines multiple de-rating, higher capex, lower gross margins and demand deceleration; bull requires better operating leverage rather than price momentum. Where bear/bull are only illustrative, they are clearly labeled above. Avoid adding probability-weighted outputs without independently supported scenario probabilities.
