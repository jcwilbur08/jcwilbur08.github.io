 Executive View
 ASML is one of the rare public companies where the business quality is easier to underwrite than the stock entry point. The
 company remains the sole supplier of EUV lithography systems, sits at the choke point of leading-edge logic and advanced
 DRAM capacity, and has moved from a 2025 digestion year into a 2026 acceleration cycle driven by AI-related customer
 capacity plans. The investment question is not whether ASML is a superior franchise; it is whether the current ADR price near
 $1,700 already discounts too much of the next five years.

       Portfolio Verdict
       Research posture: Add, but staged. For genuinely long-term new money, the current $1,680-$1,720 range is
       acceptable for a small starter or incremental add because the company has raised 2026 guidance, has net cash, and
       retains monopoly-like EUV economics. It is not a full-position entry with a large margin of safety. I would treat below
       $1,550 as a better add zone and below $1,450 as a Strong Add zone, assuming no thesis damage. Above roughly
       $1,900, I would wait for either a pullback or another visible step-up in 2027-2028 estimates.

 What Has Changed
 The biggest change since prior ASML debates is the magnitude and durability of the AI-driven capacity cycle. ASML entered
 2026 guiding to EUR 34-39B of revenue and 51-53% gross margin, then raised guidance after Q1 to EUR 36-40B, and after Q2
 to EUR 43-45B with 54-56% gross margin. That is not a small beat; it is a material reset of the earnings base.

 Business Model And Moat
 ASML sells and services the lithography systems that semiconductor manufacturers use to pattern chips. Its most important
 franchise is EUV, the technology required for the most advanced logic nodes and advanced memory roadmaps. The company
 also sells DUV systems, metrology and inspection products, computational lithography, and a large installed-base service and
 field-option stream.

  - EUV monopoly: ASML is the only scaled supplier of EUV lithography tools, making it structurally different from most
    semiconductor equipment peers.

  - Customer lock-in: leading-edge foundries and memory manufacturers plan node roadmaps around ASML system availability,
    productivity, and upgrade cadence.

  - Installed base compounding: service and field options reached EUR 8.2B in 2025, up 26.2%, and should grow as more
    EUV/DUV tools are deployed.

  - Supplier ecosystem depth: optics, light sources, precision mechanics, and software are extremely difficult to replicate quickly;
    the Carl Zeiss relationship is strategically important.

  - Main moat risk: China is trying to localize DUV capability, and export controls can alter mix, timing, and addressable market,
    particularly for China-related demand.

 Five-Year Financial Deep Dive
 ASML has compounded revenue from EUR 18.6B in 2021 to EUR 32.7B in 2025, while keeping gross margin above 50% and
 sustaining very high returns on equity and invested capital. Free cash flow is lumpy because customer advances, working
 capital, and system shipment timing can move sharply, but the five-year cash generation profile remains excellent.

 Owner earnings proxy: free cash flow, defined as operating cash flow less capital expenditures, is the cleanest practical
 owner-earnings proxy here. The limitation is that ASML working capital can swing with customer advances and shipment
 timing, so single-year FCF yields can mislead. The better test is multi-year FCF conversion across a cycle.

 Growth Drivers
  - Advanced logic: AI accelerators, HPC, mobile, and 2nm/1.4nm roadmaps require more EUV exposures and higher productivity
    systems.

  - Advanced DRAM and HBM: AI memory demand supports lithography intensity in DRAM, not just foundry logic.

  - High-NA EUV: EXE systems are early, expensive, and adoption timing remains a debate, but successful ramp would extend
    ASML leadership into future nodes.

  - Installed base management: tool utilization, productivity upgrades, and field options turn the installed base into a
    compounding revenue stream.

  - 2030 model: management continues to frame a long-term opportunity of EUR 44-60B revenue and 56-60% gross margin,
    though the current 2026 guide already approaches the low end of that revenue framework.

 Valuation And Entry Level
 The current price is not obviously cheap on trailing metrics. At $1,711.32, ASML trades around 53.8x trailing earnings, 56.7x
 trailing FCF, and 16.2x sales on third-party market data. The more relevant measure is forward earnings: consensus FY 2026
 EPS is EUR 38.33 and forward P/E is roughly 39x. That multiple is high, but less extreme if 2027 EPS can move toward the low
 EUR 50s and if gross margin stays in the mid-to-high 50s.

 Decision hinge: the stock works from here if 2026 is not the peak of the AI-driven order cycle and if ASML can convert higher
 output into durable 2027-2028 earnings rather than a one-year capacity catch-up. If the market starts treating 2026 as peak
 earnings, the current multiple is vulnerable.

 Risks And Red Flags
  - Export controls: ASML disclosed that China represented 29.1% of 2025 net sales, down from 36.1% in 2024, and export rules
    can affect system mix, shipment timing, and service availability.

  - China localization: indigenous DUV progress would not replace EUV soon, but it could pressure mature-node DUV economics
    and China mix over time.

  - Customer concentration and cyclicality: TSMC, Samsung, Intel, and large memory customers can change capex quickly if AI
    demand pauses or customer commitments loosen.

  - High-NA adoption risk: High-NA is strategically important but customer adoption curves, economics, and timing remain
    uncertain.

  - Supply chain bottlenecks: ASML is increasing capacity, but optics, precision components, and field-installation complexity
    constrain output.

  - Valuation risk: a great company can still disappoint if a 39x forward P/E multiple meets flat or falling estimates.

 Portfolio Fit
 Using retirement-portfolio-47.xlsx as the sole portfolio source, ASML is already present but immaterial: 2 ADRs, $3,359.84
 market value, and about 0.20% of the portfolio. That size is small enough that adding one or two shares over time would not
 create concentration risk, but the ADR price makes position-building lumpy. Because your portfolio already has meaningful
 mega-cap technology and AI-adjacent exposure through AMZN, AAPL, MSFT, GOOG, META, AVGO, and NVDA, ASML should be
 treated as a high-quality semiconductor infrastructure holding, not as a need-to-own-at-any-price AI trade.

 Thesis-Break Conditions
  - Net bookings roll over for two consecutive quarters without a clear timing explanation, especially in EUV.

  - 2027 revenue expectations fall below the 2026 run-rate, suggesting the current year is peak rather than base-building.

  - Gross margin drops below 52% despite strong revenue, indicating mix, pricing, or execution deterioration.

  - Export controls materially impair service, DUV, or upgrade economics beyond management guidance.

  - High-NA customer adoption is delayed by more than a year or economics look inferior to multi-patterning alternatives.

  - The ADR trades above $2,100 without matching estimate upgrades, pushing the position from Add to valuation-risk Watch.

 Conclusion
 ASML is a top-tier compounder with an unusually strong strategic position, a net-cash balance sheet, excellent returns, and a
 visible AI-driven demand reset. The current price range is not a bargain, but it is a defensible place to add modestly for a
 10-year investor who is building a position slowly. My conclusion is Add, staged, with a preference to buy in small increments
 now and reserve larger capital for pullbacks below $1,550 or for another clear upward reset in 2027-2028 earnings visibility.
