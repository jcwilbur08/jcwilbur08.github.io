00 / Earnings-call incremental evidence

Official prepared remarks, pp. 2–17; performance review; November 24. Management claims are not independent verification. Q&A unavailable.

Margin normalization, not a new demand warning

Q3 non-GAAP operating margin reached 14.2%, +490 bp year over year; gross margin was 21.2%, +10 bp, with operating costs at 7% of sales. Management attributed the expected sequential ISG margin moderation to a less favorable North American mix; scale efficiencies and own-IP storage support durable profitability. Q4 guidance implies $56.25B sales, $23.5B AI-server revenue, roughly 120% ISG growth and $8.40 adjusted EPS. Do not annualize Q3 peak margins.

Credit discipline and deployment

Management describes credit checks at order acceptance and before production, with selective short-duration DFS bridge funding. Orders are screened for financing, end users, compliance and site readiness. Rack integration and pre-testing may improve deployment timing. These practices reduce risk but do not eliminate receivable impairments, cancellations or land/power delays.

00b / Financing, guidance and valuation response

Shareholder return: a more nuanced balance-sheet view

Management reports $16.3B cash and investments and 0.7x core leverage (company definition, distinct from consolidated net debt). It also reports 9.0M shares repurchased at $488 average during Q3, roughly $4.39B, above the current $302.55 price. The additional $25B buyback authorization is flexibility, not a guaranteed spend. At today's price, $25B would notionally purchase 82.6M shares, about 13% of the pre-print share count, before issuance and price changes. Management's policy targets at least 80% of adjusted FCF, not conventional FCF. YTD cash-statement payouts ~$11.07B vs conventional FCF $7.58B remain a funding checkpoint; management's shareholder-return presentation rounds to $11.2B.

03 / Valuation, DCF and entry discipline

At $302.55, FY27 guided adjusted P/E 10.19x and GAAP P/E 10.71x; FY28 prior consensus $30.41 (Nov 12 thesis record, stale) implies 10.0x. Hypothetical FY28 normalized EPS $30–34 yields 8.9–10.1x. Dividend run rate $2.52 implies ~0.83%. Five-year average forward P/E reported ~11.8x before earnings; historical and current peer multiples are not necessarily directly comparable. HPE and SMCI are hardware peers; Nvidia is a supplier with distinct economics. A normalized PEG cannot be reliably calculated from the extraordinary AI ramp; avoid using single-year growth. Annualized nine-month conventional FCF ~$10.1B implies ~19.0x price/FCF using 635.81M shares; annualization is illustrative, not a forecast.
