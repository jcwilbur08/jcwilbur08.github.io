Sector-level selling

Reuters describes broad AI-equity pressure around Nvidia leadership disruption and the market's reassessment of capex economics ahead of Nvidia earnings. This is not direct evidence that TSM wafer orders have been canceled. [7]

TSM-specific transmission

Reuters reported new US curbs targeting access to advanced AI chips for Chinese entities, potentially adding foundry compliance and licensing requirements. Customer-mix impact is not quantified. [6]

Contrarian test: It would be a mistake to dismiss the decline as “just sentiment.” A demand pause by large AI customers, tighter controls on China-linked designs or higher capital intensity could impair 2027 EPS / FCF even if October revenue looks strong. Today's prices can reflect information not yet present in monthly disclosures.

What is *not* new

Leading-edge process leadership, a high HPC mix, robust profitability, rising N2 wafer shipments and a strong balance sheet remain structurally intact. Therefore this refresh updates the price, event risk, valuation and add thresholds rather than restating unchanged manufacturing history.

03 / Business and industry

A manufacturing bottleneck with significant pricing power.

TSMC is the leading independent foundry for high-performance logic chips. Its differentiation rests on process R&D, yields, ecosystem enablement, scale, advanced packaging and a global customer design backlog. No single rival currently reproduces the full leading-edge manufacturing and execution ecosystem at comparable scale.

Technology mix / Q3

79%

7nm-and-below share of wafer revenue

2nm 8% · 3nm 33% · 5nm 28% · 7nm 10%. N2 rose from 3% in Q2 to 8% of Q3 wafer revenue. [1,3]

Platform / Q3

68%

High performance computing share of sales

Smartphone 21%; IoT 4%; auto 4%; DCE 1%. HPC revenue grew 22% QoQ. [3]

Durable advantages

  • Yield and learning-curve effects: customer designs and process optimization reinforce effective cost per usable transistor.
  • Complexity and switching cost: design enablement, packaging and multi-year product schedules make unplanned transfers expensive.
  • Scale: advanced-node fab utilization and R&D spending benefit from broad hyperscaler, CPU, GPU and handset demand.
  • Geographic expansion: US and other overseas fabs may improve customer resilience and policy positioning, but come with material profit dilution.

Strongest competing arguments

Samsung's foundry capability, Intel's foundry ambitions, incremental chiplet modularity, alternate packaging solutions, and customer ASIC design could limit pricing power over time. GPU/AI exposure also creates concentration in capex cycles even when end-customer names diversify. Geopolitical manufacturing concentration is the most material nonfinancial risk.

Why gross margin can decline in a healthy business

Management attributed the Q3 130bp sequential gross-margin decline primarily to the 2nm ramp. Overseas fab dilution is expected at ~2 percentage points for full-year 2026, potentially widening to 3–4pp over the next several years. These are real earnings and cash-flow drags; they do not necessarily signal lost pricing power. [3]

Management and allocation judgment

The high-reinvestment strategy fits the secular-demand outlook; however, the burden is unusually large. Management reiterates progressively higher cumulative 2026–28 investment versus the prior three years and commits to sustainable growing cash dividends. Buybacks are not central to the thesis. The key accountability metric is incremental FCF and ROIC per $1 of capacity capex, especially in foreign fabs.

What could go wrong: the market may be right that AI systems monetization and financing do not support hyperscaler capex beyond 2027. TSMC can report strong current utilization while its normalized long-horizon cash flow valuation remains impaired.

Therefore a simplistic average peer P/E is less informative than TSM's forward EPS, normalized FCF and its own historical EV multiples. A decision-grade same-period normalized foundry peer dataset is not verified in this update.

Reverse DCF: what must go right?

Holding the discount rate at 9.0%, terminal growth 3.5% and second-five-year growth at half of first-five-year growth, the $356.70 share price implies ~15.0% annual FCF growth in years 1–5 if starting normalized FCF is $50B; ~16.8% if it starts at reported trailing $45B. This is a demanding absolute cash-growth requirement, even if forecast EPS growth looks cheap.

Reconciliation: Earnings-multiple base ~$515 assumes 22× the $23.39 FY27 EPS estimate; DCF base ~$421 explicitly accounts for future heavy capex and discount rates. Neither is a promise of a return. The divergence is why additions should be staged and conditioned on cash conversion.

DCF formula: PV[FCFₜ/(1+r)ᵗ] for years 1–10 + PV[FCF₁₀(1+g)/(r−g)] + net cash, divided by ADR-equivalent shares. Terminal value is material (~67% of base enterprise value), so modest changes in r, terminal g or capex produce large target swings. This is the primary source of intrinsic-value uncertainty.

Dated checkpoints

  • Throughout 2027: N2 capacity/yields, HPC share, overseas dilution, 2027 capital budget and FCF.

Explicit draft thesis-break conditions — require CIO review

  1. Two successive quarters of material leading-edge demand deceleration, verified customer deferrals and advanced-node utilization erosion.
  2. Persistent N2 qualification or yield failures, or documented loss of key leading-edge design wins.
  3. Gross margin below 55% for two quarters from structural deterioration rather than a transient node ramp or currency effect.
  4. Multi-year normalized FCF stagnation despite rising profits and consistently elevated capex; no improving return on invested capital.
  5. A material sustained Taiwan production disruption or binding export rules that substantially reduce served addressable demand.
Open questions: How much FY2027 capex is contractually committed? What portion of October's growth represents N2 capacity rather than price/mix? What is TSM-specific revenue exposure to the newest US export controls? How much of the FY2027 analyst EPS forecast is already embedded in customer wafer reservations? These cannot be reliably answered from available public reporting.
11 / Audit trail and conclusion

Research conclusion and source ledger.

TSM has a rare process-and-scale moat, high incremental demand and financial resilience. At roughly 15.25× estimated FY2027 EPS, valuation is considerably more favorable than four days earlier. The caveat is meaningful: the same stock is roughly 38× trailing free cash flow, geopolitical losses are nonlinear, and the sector's largest customer catalyst is imminent.
