Do not chase above a defined gate, and do not initiate new adds inside roughly one week of earnings. A high rank identifies the best next-dollar destination; it does not override entry discipline.

Bottom line

NVDA is the cleanest incremental dollar. It carries 8/10 conviction, sits inside the $215–235 staged-add band, remains only 0.57% of portfolio value, and has no imminent earnings event.
