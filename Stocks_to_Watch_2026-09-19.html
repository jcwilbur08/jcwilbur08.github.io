Decision rules

Use catalysts to update evidence, not to force trades

NVDA / AVGO / ASML
Positive: strong demand, intact capex budgets, improving cash conversion, no material new export constraint.
Negative: customer financing grows faster than cash generation, capex pauses, or trade restrictions broaden.
GEHC / UNH
Positive: improving utilization/margin commentary, hospital budgets remain durable, reimbursement visibility improves.
Negative: utilization reaccelerates or hospital capex slows materially.
JPM / GS / AXP
Positive: benign credit, resilient premium spending and stronger deal pipeline.
Negative: consumer delinquencies or capital-markets guidance weaken as funding costs rise.
AMT
Positive: long yields retreat without a growth scare.
Negative: 10-year establishes above ~5.1%–5.2% and financing spreads widen.

Capital-allocation hierarchy

Portfolio fit first: AVUV and VXUS remain the cleanest diversification adds. Direct-stock opportunity second: NVDA, AVGO, ASML and GEHC become more attractive when volatility creates a larger margin of safety. Do not chase: GEV and other long-duration infrastructure winners when valuation support is thin despite strong fundamentals.
