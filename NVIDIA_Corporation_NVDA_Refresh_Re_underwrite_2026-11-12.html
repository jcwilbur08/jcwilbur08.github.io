02 / Jensen Huang: what is actually at risk?

What does not disappear overnight

The CUDA software ecosystem, the Vera Rubin hardware roadmap, installed networking and inference workflows, booked near-term customer commitments, and relationships run by engineering and sales teams remain institutional assets. Kress has been CFO since 2013. Nvidia says its operations and customer commitments should continue without interruption; this is management’s assurance, not yet independent proof.

What could deteriorate

Huang personally integrated architecture, ecosystem investments, TSMC and memory supplier negotiations, major hyperscaler negotiations and fast product decisions. A replacement may preserve the product but lose negotiating leverage or decision speed. Dual interim CEO/CFO duties and an open CEO search add execution risk precisely as the business scales.

Legal / national-security uncertainty: Reporting describes allegations involving private relationships and suspected intelligence links. These claims require investigation; the allegation that company information was compromised is not an established fact. Nvidia says it has no reason to believe information or national security was compromised. A confirmed export-control, disclosure, compliance or counterparty violation would materially change the downside model. See AP reporting and Nvidia announcement.

Valuation transmission: Our base case applies a 10% discount rate instead of extrapolating the prior premium market multiple. The bear case imposes weaker FCF, 11.5% discounting, and only 2.5% terminal growth. The largest danger is not lost sales this week; it is a multiyear reduction in competitive advantage, partner economics or regulatory access that consensus estimates do not yet reflect.

03 / Franchise quality, industry and competitive dynamics

Company Quality — Exceptional operating franchise, now elevated governance risk (8/10). Nvidia sells far more than GPUs: accelerator systems, NVLink interconnects, networking switches, CPUs, data-center software, system architecture and developers’ tooling. Integrated performance, time-to-deploy and CUDA lock-in are the moat. In Q2 FY27, Data Center was ~$89.0B of $96.2B total revenue (~92.5%), making the growth engine extremely concentrated. Nvidia Q2 earnings release.

Demand: Q2 revenue grew 106% year over year; Q3 management guide was $108B ±2%, with no China Data Center compute revenue assumed. Vera Rubin production and high-bandwidth-memory supply are the immediate execution tests. Corporate AI utilization and hyperscaler investment remain supportive, but an AI capex slowdown would hit Nvidia disproportionately.

Competitive threat: AMD accelerators, Broadcom/custom ASICs, Google TPU-class systems, hyperscaler in-house silicon and potentially lower-cost inference paths can erode Nvidia pricing power. The realistic challenge is selective workload migration plus margin pressure, not instant ecosystem replacement. Power, permitting, debt-funded data center construction, elevated long yields and GPU financing can slow customer deployments independently of AI-model progress.

Reverse DCF — what is the stock pricing?

At $166.19, the equity price is ~$4.01T. Holding the base discount rate, 3% terminal growth, share count and relative 10-year FCF shape fixed, the current price requires only ~80% of base-case future FCF: ~$ 148B FY27 and ~$ 204B FY28, versus our base $185B/$255B. This is a reverse-scaling identity—not proof that the market specifically forecasts those annual amounts. If the entire FCF curve instead deteriorates and the cost of equity rises, the implied expectations are harsher.

Thesis-break triggers: (1) confirmed material compromise or export-control misconduct with durable commercial/financial consequences; (2) two consecutive material Data Center misses not plausibly due to timing; (3) gross margins structurally below ~70%; (4) sustained normalized FCF conversion below ~60% from receivables/customer financing rather than tax or timing; (5) major partner order cancellations or widespread viable ASIC substitutions; (6) actual losses from guarantees and debt-supported demand, rather than only disclosed maximum contingent exposure.

Open questions: How will the board independently validate export controls, conflicts and information security? Who retains technical roadmap authority? Will Kress remain CFO after a permanent appointment? Will Rubin volumes/mix and gross margins meet guidance? How much of $279B of commitments is backed by cancelable versus firm customer orders? How quickly does net working capital reverse? What is the portfolio-wide look-through AI concentration in mutual funds?

Decision: A 10.4× FY28 consensus earnings multiple is hard to ignore for an AI compute franchise of this caliber.
