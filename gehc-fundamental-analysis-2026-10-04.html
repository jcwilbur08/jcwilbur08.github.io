Executive Summary

Investment conclusion

Nothing in the last two days changed the thesis; the label did. GE HealthCare's October 2 note carried a legacy-scale ADD with a $92 base value, ~43% upside from $64.29, and conviction 7/10. Under the new rating scale effective October 3, conviction 7/10 combined with ≥10% expected upside is a Buy — not Strong Buy, because conviction is 7 rather than 8–10. The base value is unchanged at $92 (~18.8x the $4.90 midpoint of reaffirmed 2026 adjusted EPS guidance), the $62 accumulation gate installed October 2 stays live toward ~0.5% portfolio weight, and the fundamentals that supported the call — a record $23.9B backlog, reaffirmed guidance, a 13x multiple — are intact. This is a re-rating translation with a fresh two-day news check, not a new thesis.

The re-rating: translating ADD into the new scale

The October 2 note rated GEHC ADD under the legacy scale — a judgment that already embedded conviction 7/10, a $92 base value, and ~43% upside. Under the new scale, that judgment maps mechanically to Buy. No fundamental input changed; only the ruler did.

Why conviction stays 7/10
  • Record orders, record backlog, reaffirmed guidance — the core thesis is intact.
  • But the PCS strategic review is unresolved, the CFO is interim, China is assumed down for 2026, and a securities-fraud investigation continues. That is not a buy-with-size setup.
  • Conviction 7 means positive with identified risks still outstanding — exactly the Buy zone.

Translation, not escalation. The legacy ADD already meant "accumulate, don't chase" — that staged-entry nuance now lives in the gate language (accumulate ≤$62 toward ~0.5%), not in the rating. The Buy rating says the expected return is strong; the gate says how to earn it patiently.

06 | Risks & Catalysts

Risks and catalysts into year-end

Key risks
  • PCS strategic review. The swing factor: a dilutive outcome or a decision to retain a bleeding business extends the overhang.
  • China. Sales assumed down for 2026; anti-corruption procurement crackdown, VBP pricing, local competition.
  • Q3 print (~Oct 28, estimated). A miss or guide cut at 13x would still hurt — cheap can get cheaper on new negatives.
  • Tariffs and cost inflation. ~$250M gross headwind in the guide; Section 232 probe overhang.
  • Interim CFO. Guidance credibility is discounted until a permanent appointment.
  • Litigation. Securities-fraud investigations (Glancy Prongay and now Frank R. Cruz) over the April Q1 disclosure; no suits filed yet.
Catalysts
  • Q3 earnings (~Oct 28, estimated) — test of PCS sequential improvement and backlog conversion.
  • RSNA Nov 29–Dec 3 — the radiology showcase; imaging product momentum on display.
  • FDA decisions — StarGuide GX and Vivid portfolio 510(k)s.
  • PCS review resolution — removal of the single biggest overhang.
  • Permanent CFO appointment — restores guidance credibility.
  • Dividend compounding — third straight annual hike; $500M buyback remaining.
