5. Underwriting read-through: highest-impact positions

TSM

The strongest fundamental dislocation

At $322, TSM trades at ~13.8× the Nov 17 working 2027 ADR EPS estimate of $23.39. This EPS input is not a newly refreshed sell-side consensus. October revenue rose 59.7% year over year, supporting the view that AI-related wafer demand has not yet collapsed. Prior earnings-scenario base was ~$515; separate DCF base ~$421. The major countercase is a capex/financing-led industry correction plus Taiwan/geopolitical exposure.

Start $315–330. Add more $285–305 only if utilization, N2 yield, and leading-edge demand remain sound. Research conviction 9/10; size deliberately given Taiwan tail risk. [S4] [S5] [S8]

ADBE

Value with a measurable cash-flow hurdle

At ~$222, the Nov 19 reviewed SBC-adjusted equity DCF base is ~$311 and weighted value ~$290. The reverse DCF implied low long-run economic FCF growth, but this is only valuable if Adobe can defend Creative seats, pricing and organic ARR from AI-native workflows. The stock is stable today, meaning this is a valuation opportunity rather than a new one-day capitulation.

Starter $215–225, stronger $195–210; confirm organic net-new ARR and SBC-adjusted FCF at the next print. Conviction 8.5/10. [S9]

ASML

Quality moat at a discount

ASML fell from an October research price ~$1,615 to ~$1,276, improving the modeled cushion against a ~$1,741 older base DCF. The model is particularly sensitive to EUV/High-NA shipment conversion, gross margins, and customer-prepayment cash-flow adjustments. China curbs and customer financing concerns remain risks.

Starter $1,240–1,290; more compelling $1,110–1,200. Conviction 8.5/10; small top-up only until order quality is verified. [S11]

NVDA

Compelling nominal valuation, unusual idiosyncratic risk

The ~$132 print is ~14.1× an earlier FY2027 adjusted EPS estimate of $9.34 and ~8.3× earlier FY2028 $15.93. Those forecasts may be stale because the leadership exit, regulatory investigations, changes in export restrictions and customer financing merit a higher discount rate. Prior DCF bear/base/bull were $81/$216/$362, illustrating both substantial upside and heavy downside.

Only small initial add in $125–135; consider $105–120 after governance and order/backlog verification. Conviction 7.5/10, not 9 despite the apparent earnings multiple. [S2]

DELL

A valuation reset ahead of an unusually important print

The price has fallen from $371 in the Nov 12 underwrite to ~$281; at the earlier ~$25.92 FY2027 EPS input it screens near 10.8×. The prior cash DCF base was ~$396, but bear ~$160, and the near-term interpretation turns on AI server gross margin, receivables/inventory, backlog conversion, and financing risk. Dell officially reports fiscal Q3 results Tuesday Nov 24.

Do not commit a full position before the print. Conditional starter $270–285; stronger $240–260 if Q3 cash and margins validate. Conviction 7/10 pre-results. [S6]

UBER

One of the cleaner non-chip valuation opportunities

The market price ~$65 is below the previously assessed $65–72 add zone. Prior economic-FCF base value was ~$96, with substantial earnings leverage if Mobility and Delivery growth persists. It offers diversification away from semiconductor demand, although the company is still exposed to discretionary consumer activity, competition and AV economics.

Starter $63–67; stronger $56–61. Watch gross bookings, recurring FCF, partner economics and any capital-intensive autonomy model. Conviction 8.5/10. [S11]

AVGO

Strong expected profits; exposure ceiling matters

At ~$305 versus October FY2027 EPS ~$19.39, the simple forward multiple is ~15.7×. But the very high FY2027 forecast growth, AI infrastructure financing and hyperscaler concentration introduce major uncertainty. Existing direct exposure is 2.44% at the canonical export, before all fund look-through.

Starter $295–310, stronger $260–280. Consider a conservative ~3% direct position ceiling until sector/AI factor overlap is quantified. Conviction 8/10. [S7]

UNH

Separately driven defensive-sector opportunity

UNH rose ~4% today while technology declined. Prior Nov 18 probability-weighted scenario valuation was ~$399, base ~$404; stock ~$344. The thesis depends on MCR/Optum recovery and manageable Medicare Advantage/RADV-related retrospective liability; adverse audit findings could dominate headline P/E.

Starter $325–345, stronger $290–315, but avoid expanding without regulatory liability clarity. Conviction 6.5/10. [S11]

7. The countercase, scenarios and risk checkpoints

Upside / stabilization pathway
  • AI orders and bookings keep converting to revenue and cash.
  • Yield curve eases; the Fed communicates a less aggressive path.
  • Post-earnings company commentary confirms strong cash collections without funding concessions.
  • Broad indices stabilize and semiconductor losses stop broadening.
Downside / drawdown extension
  • AI capex plans slow; hyperscaler and customer financing risk grows.
  • Export-controls/geopolitical stress worsens without compensation elsewhere.
  • Revenue holds up temporarily but DSO, FCF margins or customer commitments weaken.
